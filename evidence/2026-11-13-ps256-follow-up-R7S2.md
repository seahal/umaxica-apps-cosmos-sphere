## Contract disposition

The accepted 2026-11-13 owner amendment in `adr/cosmos-jwt-ps256-and-op-signing-in-aws-kms.md` is
implemented locally: first-party JWTs use PS256 only, no ES384 overlap is accepted, and RSA
verification keys must be at least 3072 bits. Verifier checks for `typ`, `iss`, `aud`, expiry,
nonce, JTI consumption, JWKS caching/refetch and fail-closed behavior remain in place. Third-party
protocol algorithms, DPoP and WebAuthn are not changed.

Covered token families are OP-issued ID, access and logout tokens; RP `private_key_jwt` assertions;
OIDC state; Jump `rt` issuance and return-token verification; and the shared first-party JWT
keyring. JWKS publication is restricted to public RSA PS256 keys. The changed source, test and
current-document groups include:

- `lib/jit_security_jwt_{jwk,key_material,key_source,keyring,local_keyset_installer,registry}.rb`
  and the corresponding `app/adapters/oidc_op_*_verifier.rb` files.
- `app/values/{jump_rt_keyring,jump_rt_return_verifier,oidc_state_jwt,security_jwt_jump_rt_token_codec,security_jwt_rfc9068_access_token_profile}.rb`.
- OP verifier, JWK/key-material/keyring/registry, Jump, OIDC-state, JWKS publication and issuer
  boundary tests under `test/adapters/`, `test/unit/jit/security/jwt/`, `test/services/`,
  `test/values/`, `test/integration/` and `test/coverage/`.
